# Edlan & Ella Noël - Song For You

FL Studio 2026 demo project `Edlan & Ella Noël - Song For You.flp`, studied on a sandbox copy (the original is untouched). Genre as the project names it: Drum & Bass.

Liquid drum and bass at 174 bpm in C# major, made of breaks: sliced breakbeats played in order, then re-ordered ('Ghost Break'), under layered pads.

## What it does and how

**Form.** Intro, drops, breakdown, the second drop with re-ordered breaks.

**Rhythm and drums.** Slicex breaks and Fruity Slicer tops carry the groove; programmed kicks (X.........X.....) reinforce them.

**Bass.** GMS reese and a 3x Osc sub held (F2 for 12 steps, then F#2).

**Harmony.** One chord, four colours: C#6, C# sus2, C# maj7, C# sus4 repeating. The harmony moves without the root moving.

**Sounds and synths.** Pads layered from Kepler, DX10, FL Keys made crusty and run through a vocoder, FLEX Alumin Sun and FLEX Scatter Clouds strings; Sytrus octave arp in quarters; FLEX Synpluck Square; Ogun bells.

**Vocal production.** Vocal chops doubled, an ambient loop.

**Mix and sidechain.** LRA 12.2 LU, the most dynamic of the set: long quiet passages and full drops.

**Automation.** 16 clips.

**Transitions.** Impacts and risers made from the song's material.
