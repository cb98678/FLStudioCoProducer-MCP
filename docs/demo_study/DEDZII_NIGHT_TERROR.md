## What it does and how

**Form.** Intro, V1, Drop 1, V2, Mid 8, Mid 8 Ramp, Drop 2, Re-Intro, EndZII: a middle section and a ramp instead of a second build, and an ending that quotes the start.

**Rhythm and drums.** A minimal kick on every beat, closed hats on every 16th, four claps stacked as one with a ghost an 8th before the backbeat (..o.X.......X...), an open hat only on the bar's first beat, a second kick stuttering at the end of every fourth bar.

**Bass.** DEDZII TERROR (Sytrus through Gross Beat and distortion), Square Sub (Sytrus), 808 Flattened (FLEX) and Dedziiboost (Harmor an octave up) all play the same 16th ostinato.

**Harmony.** E, mostly the root and its fifth: the harmony is the bass's register and grit.

**Sounds and synths.** Crushspeak Now (FLEX), a croaking, talking lead with its high-pass and timbre automated; EQ band sweeps.

**Vocal production.** None: the croak lead does the vocal's job.

**Mix and sidechain.** Pump 7.7 dB from Gross Beat on the bass; width -13.1 dB (one of the narrowest): a mono, frontal mix.

**Automation.** 16 clips: the croak's filter and timbre, EQ sweeps.

**Transitions.** The Mid 8 Ramp climbs into Drop 2.
