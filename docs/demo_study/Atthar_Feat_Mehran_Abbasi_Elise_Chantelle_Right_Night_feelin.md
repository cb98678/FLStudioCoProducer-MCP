Pop dance at 105 bpm: one four-chord minor loop for the whole song, the energy made by parts entering, a synth bass drum and a Juno-style bass.

## What it does and how

**Form.** One long section between markers (the project marks only the end): verses and choruses on the same loop.

**Rhythm and drums.** FL's BassDrum synth as the kick, four on the floor; a sampled snare on 2 and 4; closed hats in a syncopated figure (.XXX..XX.XXX..XX); a shaker on every off-beat 8th; a perc answering late in the bar (..o...oX..X.ooXX).

**Bass.** Sytrus Juno Bass: root and the fifth below in a two-bar syncopated figure (C#3 - Ab2 - . . C#3 ...), gently pumped.

**Harmony.** C# minor: i7, v, iv7, IV sus2. The last chord turns the minor fourth major (Dorian) for a lift that never leaves the minor mood.

**Sounds and synths.** Sytrus Brass stabs, Brass Synth, Harmor Dark Pad, Dream Pad, Rhodes MK 1, a Harmor bell, muted guitar samples through overdrive and delays.

**Vocal production.** Lead vocal and features, no chop games.

**Mix and sidechain.** A Peak Controller on the kick drives the bass and synths; a Love Philter preset named Sidechain does the same job on another bus; measured pump only 1.9 dB: pop pumps gently.

**Automation.** 77 clips: filters, sends and volumes per section.

**Transitions.** Guitar and FX swells rather than risers.
