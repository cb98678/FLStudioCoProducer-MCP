## What it does and how

**Form.** 16-bar phrases; the peak is the moment every filter is open.

**Rhythm and drums.** A three-step kick (X.......X...X...), shakers on almost every 16th with accents (XX.XXX.XXX.XXX.X), wood drops and percussion sitting a third of a 16th late (a 66 % swing, a triplet feel against the straight kick), a clap pushed 0.4 of a 16th early before each beat, a FLEX GM cowbell on quarters and an agogo.

**Bass.** The bass barely plays: two quick notes on the flat second (F over E) at the end of the bar resolve to the root on the next downbeat; a GMS layer holds longer notes.

**Harmony.** E minor: i, bVII6, bVI maj7 and i, v7, bVI maj7.

**Sounds and synths.** FLEX Arp (Mobile Synth Pluck, Synpluck Dual) on a tresillo-grouped arp (E4 B4 - G4 E4+E5 B4 - G4) whose cutoff opens over 16 bars; Harmless chord pluck with its decay automated and a Fruity Filter sweep; Morphine and GMS synths; strings; Sakura.

**Vocal production.** The vocal with reverb and delay throws placed by short automation clips.

**Mix and sidechain.** Buses All Drums, Sfx, Chromatics, Bass, Synths, Pads, All Music, Mix Buss and one global reverb send; pump 2.6 dB.

**Automation.** 21 clips: the arp's cutoff over 16 bars, the pluck's decay, GMS amp release, throws.

**Transitions.** Swells and throws, no riser.
