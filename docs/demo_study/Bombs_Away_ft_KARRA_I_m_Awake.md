Big room EDM at 124 bpm: maximal layering (six claps, seven 3x Osc channels on one lead), kick changes per section, builds by ramped snare rolls.

## What it does and how

**Form.** Intro, verses with the vocal, builds, two drops, an outro: the classic two-drop club form.

**Rhythm and drums.** Six clap samples on 2 and 4 as one clap; a kick per section (Halloween, StadiumHop); an off-beat hat plus a 16th ticker on every second 16th (.X.X.X.X); snare rolls in 16ths with velocity ramping from 64 to 128; metal hits as percussion; Fruity Slicer breaks.

**Bass.** Growl bass samples played on syncopated hits (Bass Growel Notes) over 3x Osc bass layers.

**Harmony.** D minor with both a Dorian IV (G, G7) and a Phrygian bII (Eb sus2): bright and dark in four bars.

**Sounds and synths.** 3x Osc stacks (seven channels on one Layer for the lead, five on another for the bass), bounced audio layers playing the same MIDI, Toxic Biohazard high arp, DL breaker and broken leads.

**Vocal production.** Vocal cut chops pitched as a hook, plus the lead.

**Mix and sidechain.** Pump measured 2.1 dB: the loudness (-6.5 LUFS) comes from density, not from ducking.

**Automation.** 93 clips, the most of the set: filters, volumes and FX on almost every layer.

**Transitions.** Snare rolls, risers and impacts into every drop.
