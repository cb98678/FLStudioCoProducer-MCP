## What it does and how

**Form.** Intro, verse, chorus, a second verse and chorus, an outro.

**Rhythm and drums.** Kick in pairs (X..X....X..X....), the backbeat late and soft, bongos on every 16th with velocity rising and falling (3 to 100), two congas and two 'indie' percs interlocking, a low war drum at the bar's end, a jazz ride.

**Bass.** Sytrus bass and a chorused Midbass on a dotted 3-3-2 figure (Ab2 - - Ab2 - - - - Ab2 - - Ab2 - - Ab1 -), the last note dropping an octave.

**Harmony.** G minor: iv7, i7, bII maj7, bIII6, with a G7 turnaround once in four.

**Sounds and synths.** Ogun high chords (sevenths, chorused, in reverb), Morphine soft pad, a bright pad through a Love Philter, Harmor blips on a falling chromatic line, a tuned percussion line falling chromatically, risers and drops from samples.

**Vocal production.** Dry and close; a delay bank into a reverb and a reverb send, both ridden by automation; breaths dipped by 97-point volume clips.

**Mix and sidechain.** An SC bus (Limiter presets Transients and Hard Noise Gate) keyed into pads, bass and chords; a Drum Verb bus with Pitch Shifter and Blood Overdrive before the reverb; a Tonal Bus through a Frequency Shifter.

**Automation.** Frequency Shifter mix, frequency and feedback, HPF, vocal sends, breath dips.

**Transitions.** Sample drops (8-bit, reverse, static shock) at section starts.
