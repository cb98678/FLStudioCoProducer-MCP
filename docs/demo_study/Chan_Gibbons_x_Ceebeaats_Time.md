# Chan Gibbons x Ceebeaats - Time

FL Studio 2026 demo project `Chan Gibbons x Ceebeaats - Time.flp`, studied on a sandbox copy (the original is untouched). Genre as the project names it: D&B.

Liquid drum and bass at 170 bpm built around a vocal arranged like a choir: lead, doubles, harmonies, textures and ad-libs on their own buses.

## What it does and how

**Form.** A long intro (bars of sparse pads and plucks), drops with the two-step, breakdowns with the vocal stack.

**Rhythm and drums.** Kick on 1 and the 'and' of 3 (X.........X.....), snare on 2 and 4, a tambourine and an 18-inch crash on every quarter; a shuffled break loop for tops.

**Bass.** 3x Osc sine sub held under the chords; the bass bus is automated.

**Harmony.** Eb minor: an intro of bVII sus2, iv add9, bIII sus4, then i7 and bVI maj7, and a Dorian IV sus2 in the chorus.

**Sounds and synths.** FLEX plucks (Olbaid Compendium: Driving Through, Driven Distruct, Progressive Impact) octave-doubled on descending arps; GMS; pads as audio.

**Vocal production.** Lead, doubles left and right, an ambient double, a robot double through Blood Overdrive, high, mid and low harmonies, hums, 'oh's, ad-libs; buses Lead, Doubles, Harmonies, Vocal Texture, Adlib; one LuxeVerb send.

**Mix and sidechain.** Pump 1.6 dB (barely any); LRA 9.8 LU, the second most dynamic: the drops are 12 LU over the intro.

**Automation.** Bass bus and pluck volume moves.

**Transitions.** Breaks filtered in and out.
