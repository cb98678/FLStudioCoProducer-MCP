**Rhythm and drums.** Kick x...x.........x., hats on 8ths and syncopated, a 22-inch ride on quarters, tambourine and wood hits.

**Bass.** Log drum (Fruity DX10) answering late in the beat (......x---..x---) over Synpluck Cube (FLEX) and Morphine pulses in 8ths.

**Sounds and synths.** FLEX Harp, Enlightenment, Synpluck Cube, Brass Sections and Flute from the General Midi Library; Morphine basses; FL Piano.
