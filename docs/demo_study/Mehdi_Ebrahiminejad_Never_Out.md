## What it does and how

**Form.** An 8-bar drone intro, a long first section, drops at bar 33 and bar 73.

**Rhythm and drums.** Kick X.X.......X....., snare on 2 and 4, open hats on the off-beats, Fruity Slicer drum loops played chromatically as new patterns.

**Bass.** Harmor (Bajo Melbourne, Basic wobbling, Bajo Bounce) and GMS (Disco Fuzz) into one Decimate voice bus; FLEX Submarine on an octave bounce (C#3 . C#3 . C#4 .).

**Harmony.** F minor: an F sus4 to F m add9 drone, then iv, bVI, i5, V sus4.

**Sounds and synths.** FLEX Arksun Cityscape (Phoenix, Enlightenment, Dystopian Lead, Cruncher), Harmless duck stabs (minor triads), DirectWave piano for the outro.

**Vocal production.** Vocal main with delay, reverb, phaser and Soundgoodizer; setar ad-libs with their formant automated.

**Mix and sidechain.** Reverb and delay sends shared by many parts; the master only EQ, multiband and Wave Candy.

**Automation.** FLEX master filter on Phoenix and Cruncher, Love Philter on the drum loop, the setar's formant.

**Transitions.** Reverse piano chords, a wind riser, a sub impact, a reversed tombak.
