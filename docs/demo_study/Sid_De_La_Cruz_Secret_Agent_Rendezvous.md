A spy-film cue at 120 bpm that slows to 110: odd meters (7/4, 6/4, 3/4), a minor chord with a major seventh, a chromatic guitar, a harp glissando, one hall reverb.

## What it does and how

**Form.** Markers 120 Bpm, 4/4, C Min, 110 Bpm, 7/4, 6/4, 4/4, 3/4, 4/4: the form follows the scenes.

**Rhythm and drums.** A swung ride (X.xoX.xoX.xoX.xx), a hat pedal on quarters, toms, FPC wacks and a big HIT at phrase ends.

**Bass.** Harmor 'Forest' repeating one note in 16ths, Strat Mute guitar (DirectWave), Kepler AceBass.

**Harmony.** C minor: C m(maj7), Ab maj7, Bb7, Ab add9.

**Sounds and synths.** DirectWave Rhodes (C4, C5+G4, Eb5+G5+B5), Stage Grand piano stabs, Piezo guitar on D, Eb, E, FLEX harp glissando, Wave Traveller leads, DJ scratches, earthquake SFX, a moving choir.

**Vocal production.** A choir texture.

**Mix and sidechain.** One LuxeVerb Bright Hall (3 s) fed by every instrument; a Peak Controller kick sidechain; -16.4 LUFS at 19.5 dB crest: a film mix.

**Automation.** Tempo, the scratch pitch mix, the 3x Osc cutoff and resonance, Rhodes and guitar levels, a PanOMatic switch.

**Transitions.** Meter and tempo changes, hits.
