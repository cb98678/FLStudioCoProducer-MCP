## What it does and how

**Form.** Nine blocks of 8 bars.

**Rhythm and drums.** Ekit 4 Hat in grouped 16ths (xXX.xXX..xx.xxx.), Grv Snareclap on beat 3 with two ghosts (........X....oo.), vintage-kit fills (VT_SD, VT_MC, VT_CL, rimshots) in fill bars.

**Bass.** FLEX 808 Clean (Mobile Tuned 808 Bass) held, re-struck late, stepping E3 to F3; an 808 Clav sample; BooBass in a high register as a funky counter line.

**Harmony.** E pedal with F maj7 (the Phrygian bII), D add9 and C.

**Sounds and synths.** FL Keys repeating E6 in short 16ths, FLEX Just Strollin arp (Arksun Cityscape) with its cutoff wobbling over 32 beats, FLEX Creeper Drone through WaveShaper, Hyper Chorus, Love Philter and Spreader, Kepler, FLEX Driving Through as a lead.

**Vocal production.** Choir Ahh (DirectWave), a vocal with delay and Hyper Chorus.

**Mix and sidechain.** Master: two EQs, a Patcher, Blood Overdrive, an EQ, Stereo Enhancer: the master is part of the sound. LRA 1.5 LU: flat on purpose.

**Automation.** The arp's cutoff (21 points over 32 beats), Hyper Chorus, vocal mix, Love Philter.

**Transitions.** Fills and crashes.
