# Remix studies

Professional remixes the person brought, each studied whole against its original (skill remix-craft, references/study-protocol.md).
Each page gives the reading (what the remixer decided), the measurements behind it, where every line of the original went, the form as
functions, the drums, bass and harmony, the energy curve, the moves to take with their bars, and what only ears can confirm. The moves
are in the option bank (src/coproducer/data/options, sources.json "remixes") and in skills/remix-craft/references/moves.md.
