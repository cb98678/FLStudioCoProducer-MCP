# Question templates

**Melody review (restyle)**
"I transcribed the lead from the separated stem: N notes, key X, chord loop Y. Does this melody capture it? Options: 1) Use as is (recommended if it sounds right to you). 2) Clean up further (I will quantize and remove short notes). 3) Fix by hand and re-import. After you choose I will set the contour threshold you give me."

**Which of two basslines**
"Two bass options. A: simpler, sits under the kick (recommended for your target). B: busier, more movement. Which direction? I will build the other as a spare."

**Iteration cap reached**
"I made R rounds and these limits still fail: (list with numbers). Options: 1) Stop and review (recommended). 2) Allow 2 more rounds."

**Targets missing**
"The targets file still has blanks: (list). I will not guess. Options: 1) You fill them. 2) Tell me a reference track and I will measure it to propose values for you to approve."

# A/B package

Two files with the same integrated loudness, named a_<name> and b_<name>, plus a short note: what changed, before and after numbers, and which one is the new version.
