# Measure glossary

- **Integrated loudness (LUFS):** gated loudness over the whole file. What streaming targets refer to.
- **Short-term loudness:** loudness over a sliding window of a few seconds. Shows which sections are loud.
- **Loudness range (LU):** spread between quiet and loud passages.
- **True peak (dBTP):** highest level after reconstruction between samples. Needed for a safe ceiling. Sample peak can miss it.
- **Crest factor (dB):** peak minus RMS. Low means squashed.
- **Correlation:** +1 identical channels, 0 unrelated, negative means phase trouble when summed to mono.
- **Mid/side ratio:** side energy relative to mid. Higher means wider.
- **Low-end mono:** side minus mid level below a cutoff. Large positive values mean a wide low end.
- **Band energy:** level in named frequency bands (sub, bass, low mid, mid, high mid, presence, air).
