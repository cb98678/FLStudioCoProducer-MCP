## Transitions that are not risers

fx.silence_and_sub (a silent beat and a sub drop), fx.kick_filter_in (the low-pass opening is the build), fx.organic_swells (reverse
cymbals and throws), fx.snare_pitch_roll (a pitched roll and a tape stop), fx.drill_block_marks (fills and impacts at block edges),
fx.reverse_and_wind (the song's own chords reversed), fx.radio_vocal_drop (the vocal band-passed into the chorus).
