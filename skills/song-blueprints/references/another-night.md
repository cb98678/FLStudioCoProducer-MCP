| Fact | Value | Source |

## Not confirmed

- The key and chords. The breakdown gives B minor, Bm G A F#m, and calls it iv VI VII v; in B minor those chords are i VI VII v. A published chord sheet gives Am Dm G C (https://acordesweb.com/cancion/real-mccoy/another-night), and Mixgraph's key is G major. Treat the progression as a Eurodance move (harmony.eurodance_i_VI_VII_v), not as this record's chords.
- The hardware: the breakdown names a Roland JV-1080, which came out in 1994, after this 1993 single; the Korg M1 "house organ" and Juno-style bass are the sound of the era, not confirmed for this record. references/sound-recipes.md has them as era sounds.
- The mix: the dry mono rap verse and the high-passed, chorused, reverbed sung chorus (25 ms pre-delay, 2.1 s decay); peaks around -11 to -9 LUFS with the kick 6 to 8 dB over the mix. vocal.dry_verse_wide_chorus takes the space idea.
