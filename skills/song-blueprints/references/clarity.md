- The lead pumps from a ghost kick (a silent trigger) even in breakdowns without a kick. Plausible and common; not confirmed for this record.

## The structural lessons the breakdown gives (general, not about this record)

- Work in 8-bar blocks of two 4-bar phrases: the first four ask, the next four answer. The arranger's sections are already multiples of 4 and 8; keep edits that way.
- Put fills, sweeps and changes on the last beat of bar 4 or bar 8 so the ear expects the turn.
- A melody in groups of three 16ths over the four-beat pulse crosses the downbeat every bar and lines up again after three bars (48 sixteenths); resolve on bar 4.
